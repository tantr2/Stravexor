01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

FROM
FLIGHT
TO DATA.

This STRAVEXOR operation focused on the aerial mapping of a construction site in Phuket, Thailand.

The mission used the DJI Matrice 4TD to systematically capture high-resolution aerial imagery across the project area.

The captured imagery was subsequently processed into detailed mapping outputs, allowing the site to be viewed from both a conventional 2D aerial perspective and a 3D spatial perspective.
