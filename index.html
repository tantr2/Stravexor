Mission Workflow

How we operate

Every mission follows the same disciplined sequence, regardless of scale.

  1. 01

    Scope & risk assessment

    Define the area of interest, deliverables, and site-specific hazards before any flight is planned.

  2. 02

    Flight planning & clearance

    Airspace and frequency checks against CAAT and NBTC requirements, plus a standardized pre-flight checklist.

  3. 03

    Data capture

    Multi-sensor acquisition in the field — RGB, thermal, or mapping payloads depending on the mission.

  4. 04

    Processing & reporting

    Traceable processing into orthomosaics, anomaly flags, or change-detection outputs, packaged into a structured mission report.

Standards & Oversight

Operating within the rules of the sky

Flight operations are planned and flown against recognized aviation and telecom authorities.

CAAT

Civil Aviation Authority of Thailand — UAS operations compliance for every mission flown.

NBTC

National Broadcasting & Telecommunications Commission — spectrum and equipment compliance for telemetry and video links.

ICAO

International Civil Aviation Organization — operating standards aligned with international practice.

Internal Quality Audit

Missions and reporting are reviewed under an internal audit process before delivery.
